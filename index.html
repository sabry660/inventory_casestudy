Overview

Stop guessing what is in stock.

InventoryPro connects selling, buying and stock, so the numbers on screen always match the shelf.

Sales that update stock

Every invoice automatically decreases stock, in a safe database transaction.

Purchases that restock

Purchase records automatically increase stock and keep the audit trail complete.

Insight in one click

Revenue, average order, inventory value and top customers in the Reports page.

Stock flow

Every movement, accounted for.

  1. Add productsCatalog with SKU, category, price and stock
  2. PurchaseStock goes up
  3. SellInvoice created, stock goes down
  4. AuditEvery change in the movement log
Dashboard

See today’s business at a glance.

The overview shows the numbers a manager checks first, plus the products that need attention.

Key numbers

Active products, customers, today’s sales and estimated profit.

Recent sales

Latest invoices with customer, total and time.

Low-stock alerts

SKU, current stock and the alert threshold for every item that needs restocking.

Products

A catalog that stays in order.

Search, edit and control every product, and see its stock level at a glance.

  • Search by name, SKU or category
  • Add and edit products with price and category
  • Activate or disable products without deleting history
  • Stock quantity visible for every item
Sales & purchases

Transactions you can trust.

Sales invoices

Create an invoice for a customer with several line items and a discount. Stock decreases automatically.

Purchase records

Record what you buy from suppliers. Stock increases automatically.

MySQL transactions

Stock-sensitive operations run in database transactions, so inventory never ends up half updated.

Invoices

A printable invoice in one click.

Every sale opens a clean invoice with the customer, items, discount and total, ready to print.

  • Invoice number, issue date and who prepared it
  • Bill-to customer and payment summary
  • Line items with SKU, quantity, unit price and line total
  • Subtotal, discount and total
  • Print button built in
Reports

Know how the business is performing.

Reports turn the day-to-day records into the numbers owners ask for.

  • Total revenue and average order value
  • Inventory cost value and low-stock item count
  • Sales by day, with orders and revenue
  • Top customers ranked by revenue contribution
People & roles

The right access for every team member.

Admin

Full access, including staff accounts. Admins create users and reset passwords, but can never view an existing password.

Inventory Manager

Manages products, stock and purchases.

Cashier

Creates sales invoices for customers.

Customers & suppliers

Manage customers, suppliers and product categories in one People page.

Audit log

An inventory movement log records every stock change.

Staff management

Create staff users and reset passwords from the Users page.

Security

Security built into the design.

Hashed passwords

Passwords are hashed with bcryptjs and never stored or shown in plain text.

JWT authentication

Every API route is protected with JSON Web Tokens.

Role permissions

Inventory, sales and admin actions are limited by role.

Hardened API

Rate limiting, Helmet headers and prepared SQL queries reduce attack risk.
